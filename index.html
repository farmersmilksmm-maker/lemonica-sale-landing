01 / Our story

From a lemon grove dream to Young Circle.

LEMONICA opened in 2024 on N Young Circle, facing the ArtsPark in downtown Hollywood. The concept was simple and instantly readable: the lemon groves and coastal beauty of the Amalfi Coast, translated into a warm, family-owned Italian restaurant.

Everything is built around craft — pasta made daily by hand, pizza baked over open fire, signature desserts and a carefully edited wine list. Fridays and Saturdays come alive with live music, and the terrace has become one of the most photographed corners of the city.

The growth was fast. Within its first seasons LEMONICA became one of the most-reviewed restaurants in Hollywood — 2,500+ Google reviews at a 4.8 average — and a 28,000+ Instagram community grown organically through local bloggers, reels and guest content. Delivery runs on DoorDash and Uber Eats, reservations flow through OpenTable, and the brand already attracts franchise inquiries.

Today the founders are offering the business itself: a turn-key, operating, profitable restaurant with a trained team, proven recipes and a recognized brand — ready for its next owner.
